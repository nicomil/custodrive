Custodrive — verified copies for external disks
Languages: English (default), Italiano, Español.
Other installation guides: Install.it.txt, Install.es.txt.
Requirements: Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 15.4 or later.

INSTALLATION
1. Drag “Custodrive” to “Applications”.
2. Open Custodrive from Applications.

FIRST OPENING ON ANOTHER MAC
Custodrive is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate, so macOS
blocks it the first time (“the developer cannot be verified”).
To open it:
  a. Try opening it once and dismiss the alert.
  b. System Settings → Privacy & Security → at the bottom, next to
     “Custodrive was blocked”, click “Open Anyway” and confirm with your
     password.
Or, in Terminal:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Custodrive.app
This is only needed the first time.

USE
Connect an NTFS disk and select it on the left. Drag files from Finder
into the window to copy them; the first change asks for your password
once. Click “Finish Editing” or “Eject” before disconnecting the disk.
Copies are read back and verified, but an interruption can still cause
filesystem damage. Keep a backup of important data.

LANGUAGE
Choose English, Italiano or Español from the toolbar’s Language menu
(globe icon), or Custodrive → Settings. Restart to apply the choice.
